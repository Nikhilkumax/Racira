Guide

The free, unlimited AI voice generator built for the modern web

Racira TTS is a free text-to-speech tool designed for creators, students, educators and teams who need fast, reliable voice generation without the friction of API keys, monthly subscriptions or strict character limits. Type your script, choose a premium voice, and listen instantly.

An unlimited AI voice generator that respects your time

Most online TTS tools cap your usage, gate languages behind paid tiers, or force you through onboarding flows just to hear a sample. Racira TTS takes a different approach: the entire engine runs in your browser, which means there are no servers to throttle, no tokens to count, and no spreadsheets to budget.

Browser-based voice generation, end to end

Under the hood, Racira TTS uses the Web Speech API, the same speech synthesis layer that powers system voices on modern operating systems. We map our branded voices — Novaire, Atlas, Dante, Orion, Rex, Nova, Aria, Luna and Stella — to the best available voices on your device, and tune their pitch and rate so they feel consistent across browsers.

A multilingual TTS tool that adapts to your script

Whether you are writing in English, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu or Marathi, Racira TTS automatically detects the language of your text and recommends a matching voice. Switching between scripts and languages is as simple as typing.

Voice generation for YouTube, podcasts and accessibility

Creators use Racira TTS for narration drafts, faceless YouTube voiceovers, podcast intros, language learning, accessibility previews, and study material. Since everything is generated in your browser, your scripts stay private — no upload, no account, no telemetry.
